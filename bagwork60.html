Session guide

Each block begins with heavy bag work; Block 1 uses hands only. Each 15-minute block contains three 5-minute circuits. Each circuit has five 60-second stations, including transitions and recovery. The last block is 15 minutes of circuits plus a 1-minute cooldown. Stamina: aim for 45 seconds work + 15 seconds transition per station. Strength: controlled repetitions, rest as needed. Explosiveness: brief 10–15-second bursts with recovery, never continuous all-out effort.
